10.  LFU with aging -- the shift register
----------------------------------------------------------------------
    after 8 ticks: hot aging = 0xFF, cold aging = 0x00
    pass  a page used every interval saturates at 0xFF
    pass  an unused page decays to 0x00 -- LFU alone would have kept it forever on its old count
    pass  the reference bit is cleared by the tick
    pass  FrameDesc.aging mirrors PTE.aging, so victim search stays linear
    pass  one touch puts a 1 in the MSB: 0x80
    pass  and an idle interval shifts the hot page to 0x7F -- recency outranks raw frequency
